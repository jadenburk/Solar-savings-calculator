How the math works

We model each year of cost independently, then add them up to get a running total.

Edison cost in year N

Your current monthly bill × 12, then compounded by the Edison rate increase for each year you're a customer. So a $250/mo bill with 6% annual increases costs $3,000 in year 1 and roughly $3,180 in year 2.

Sunrun PPA cost in year N

Your locked-in PPA payment × 12, compounded by the PPA escalator. Sunrun's standard escalator is 2.9%, but many agreements offer 0% — adjust the slider to match the offer on the table.

Cumulative savings

The difference between the running total of Edison costs and the running total of PPA costs at each year. This is what fills the green area on the chart.

What this tool does not include

  • Time-of-use rate differences or NEM credits
  • System production changes due to weather or degradation
  • Federal tax credits (PPAs are not owned by the customer)
  • Home resale impact

Numbers are illustrative — your signed agreement governs actual payments.
